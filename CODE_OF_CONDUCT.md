# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Specadia community respectful and harassment-free,
regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials or private design artifacts.
- Focus technical disagreement on evidence and project goals.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's
private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a channel listed on their GitHub
profile. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
